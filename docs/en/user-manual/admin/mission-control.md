# Mission Control

Mission Control is the live dashboard of a conference. It's where Admins and Team Members land when they open a conference, and it works well on a big screen in the Secretariat or a hallway. Delegates, Non-state Actors and Spectators are sent to the participant view instead.

![The Mission Control dashboard with committee cards and insight tiles](shot:admin/mission-control-dashboard)

:::live admin/mission-control

The top bar shows the current time and the menu with **Mission Control**, **Attendance**, **Statistics** and, for Admins, **Configuration**.

## Committee cards

Every committee gets a card with its abbreviation, name, active agenda item and current status with its countdown.

- Committees in Formal Debate come first, sorted by abbreviation.
- Committees in Informal Caucus, Moderated Informal Caucus, Pause or Suspension follow, the one ending soonest first.
- A card blinks red once 2 minutes or less are left, or when the time is already up. That's your cue to get people back into the room.
- Click a card to open that committee's chair view.

## Insight tiles

Next to the committees, tiles show what's happening across the conference. A tile only appears when it has data to show.

**Right now**

- **Votes in progress**: committees with an open vote.
- **Speaking now**: who holds the floor in each committee.
- **Speakers list**: the queue of a committee with an open, non-empty speakers list.
- **Attendance**: share of delegates present, for example "42 of 56 delegates present".

**Today**

- **Today so far**: **Speeches today**, **Votes held**, **Resolutions adopted** and **Debate time**.
- **Most active right now**, **Closest vote today**, **Longest speech today** and **Vote outcomes today**.

**Overall**

- **Recently adopted**: the latest adopted resolutions.
- **Committee activity**: speaking time per committee.
- **Speaking time by region** and **Speaking time by type**.
- **Resolution status** and **Amendment activity**.
- **Papers awaiting vote** and **Papers per committee**.
- **Conference at a glance**: **Delegates**, **Committees**, **Speeches (all-time)** and **Resolutions (all-time)**.

Committee data updates live. The statistics refresh every minute, and the tiles reshuffle every 45 seconds so a wall display stays interesting. Charts may switch between chart types when they move.

When a committee adopts a resolution, Mission Control celebrates with confetti and a banner for 45 seconds.
