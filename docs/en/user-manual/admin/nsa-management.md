# NSA Management

The **Non-state Actors** tab lists every NSA and UN actor registered for the conference, alongside their icon and role type.

To add one, fill in a **Name**, choose the **Role** (**Non-state Actor** or **UN Actor**), and optionally an **Icon**, a Font Awesome icon name such as `building-ngo`, to represent them visually instead of a flag. Click **Add NGO** to save.

Entries can't be edited. To change one, remove it with the trash icon (asks for confirmation) and add it again.

![The Non-state Actors tab, listing the General Secretary and ICRC with their role types](shot:admin/nsa-tab)

Unlike delegations, NSAs and UN actors aren't tied to a single committee. They're conference-wide, since the same person may move between committees during a conference.

## People and attendance codes

To give someone access as an NSA, add them in [Users & Roles](./users-roles) with the **Non-state Actor** role and link them to the NSA. Each person with that role gets their own attendance code. Chairs scan it to check them in and out of whichever committee sessions they attend (see [Roll Call & Attendance](../chair/roll-call-attendance)).

You print the badges from **Attendance**, tab **By NSA**. It lists every NSA person with their attendance code and current committee. Use **QR card** for a single badge, **Print all cards** for the whole set, or **Export CSV** for a list.
