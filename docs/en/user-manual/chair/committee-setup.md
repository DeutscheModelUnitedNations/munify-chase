# Committee Setup

The **Set up** page (Alt+1) is your committee's control panel. Check it before your session starts.

![The chair Set up page, showing Change status, State of Debate, Agenda item, and Presentation View sections](shot:chair/setup-page)

:::live chair/setup

The left column shows the current agenda item, state of debate and status, plus a **Majorities** card. It lists how many delegations are present, the current simple and two-thirds majorities, and how many sponsors a paper needs (10% of those present, rounded up). The same cards appear on the other chair pages.

## Set Status / State of Debate

The **Change status** and **State of Debate** cards embed the same controls you get with Alt+S and Alt+D. See [Agenda & State of Debate](./agenda-status) for details on both.

## Agenda Item

A dropdown lists every agenda item, and a **+** button lets you add a new one by typing its title. Switching or adding an agenda item is instant, with no confirmation needed.

## Presentation Mode

The **Presentation View** card controls the shared screen:

- **Open Presentation View** (Alt+P) opens or refocuses the shared screen meant for a projector.
- **Enter Fullscreen** appears on platforms that support it and works once the presentation window is open.
- **Layout**: choose **Default Layout** or **Layout for Small Screens**, depending on your room's display.
- Two font size sliders (10 to 50, previewed live): one for the base font size and one for the resolution text. These are separate, so you can size the resolution text differently from everything else on screen.
- **Display Regional Blocs**: toggle whether regional-group breakdowns appear on the shared screen.

See [Presentation Mode](./presentation-mode) for what actually shows up automatically as your session progresses.

## Self-add to Speakers List

Toggle whether delegates and non-state actors can add themselves to speakers lists directly, instead of you adding everyone manually. It is off by default. Check with your Secretariat if you're unsure which is expected.

## Requests

The **Requests** card turns digital requests on or off: "Allow delegates and non-state actors to send requests (e.g. points of order or motions) to the chairs." It is off by default. When you turn it on, a **Requests** tab appears in the dock and delegates get a **Requests** card on their committee page. See [Requests](./requests).

Neither this card nor **Self-add to Speakers List** is shown in the offline demo.

## Whiteboard

A preview of the committee's shared whiteboard, with an **Edit** button. See [Whiteboard](./whiteboard) for how to write, upload images, and add a QR code.
