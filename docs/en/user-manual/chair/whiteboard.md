# Whiteboard

Every committee has a shared whiteboard you can use to put something in front of the whole room: a schedule, an uploaded image, or a scannable link.

## Editing

From the **Set up** page, click **Edit** on the whiteboard card to open the editor, a rich text surface with a formatting toolbar (headings, lists, etc.) and a floating menu for quick formatting when you select text.

![The whiteboard editor modal, with a formatting toolbar and an empty writing area](shot:chair/whiteboard-editor)

Two special tools:

- **Picture**: insert an image, either with **Upload** from your device or from a **URL**, then **Insert image**.
- **Generate QR-Code**: insert a scannable QR code. Choose **To Committee** to link to your committee (**To participant view** or **To the working papers**), or **Custom URL** to link anywhere else, then click **Use QR-Code**.

When you're done, click **Publish changes** to push it live, or **Abort** to discard your edits.

## Visibility

On participants' dashboards the whiteboard appears as soon as it has content and disappears when you clear it. On the presentation screen, the **Default Layout** and **Layout for Small Screens** always include a whiteboard tile, which shows a placeholder while the whiteboard is empty. When a draft resolution is active, the presentation screen switches to the resolution layout, which has no whiteboard.
