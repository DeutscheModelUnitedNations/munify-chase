# Committee Overview & Status

## The committee overview grid

Non-state actors and spectators see a grid of every committee in the conference after opening it from the launcher. Each tile shows the committee's name, abbreviation, active agenda item and current status at a glance, so you can quickly find where you need to be without opening each one. Delegates skip this grid and go straight to their own committee.

## Understanding committee status

Chairs move a committee through five statuses over the course of a session. As a participant, the status tells you what kind of interaction is expected of you right now:

| Status                        | What it means for you                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Formal debate**             | The general speakers list is running. Delegates can request the floor and speeches happen one at a time.           |
| **Moderated informal caucus** | A structured, chair-set discussion on a specific sub-topic, with its own speaker queue and shorter speaking times. |
| **Informal meeting**          | Free-form time for delegates to negotiate directly. No speakers list is active.                                    |
| **Suspension**                | The session is paused (e.g. a break). Nothing is expected of you until it resumes.                                 |
| **Pause**                     | A brief hold, often between agenda items or while the chair sets something up.                                     |

Each status can carry a headline (e.g. the caucus topic) and a countdown if the chair set a duration. Both are shown on your dashboard.

## Agenda items

A committee can have multiple items on its agenda, but only one is active at a time. The active agenda item's title is shown on your dashboard and drives which speakers list, resolutions, and votes are currently relevant.

## The shared presentation screen

Most committee rooms display a shared **presentation view** on a projector. You don't interact with it directly, but it's helpful to know what it shows, since it's the room's shared source of truth:

- The current speaker and their remaining time
- The speakers queue
- Regional group / bloc breakdowns, if enabled
- The active resolution (during resolution work) or roll call / voting results, depending on what the chair is running

If you're ever unsure what's currently happening, glance at the projector. It reflects the same live status your own dashboard shows.

## Majorities at a glance

Your dashboard's **Majorities** card shows four numbers, based on who's currently marked present. Hover or tap a tile to see what it means.

- **Count of Present Countries**: how many delegations are marked present.
- **Simple majority**: the votes needed for more in favor than against.
- **Two-thirds majority**: the votes needed for a two-thirds majority.
- **Amendment sponsors**: how many supporting delegations an amendment needs before it can be submitted (see [Amendments](./amendments)).

These numbers update automatically as delegates check in or leave, so they always reflect the room as it stands right now.
