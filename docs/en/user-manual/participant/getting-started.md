# Getting Started as a Participant

This guide is for anyone taking part in a committee session as a **Delegate**, **Spectator**, or **Non-State Actor (NSA)**, collectively "participants." If you're chairing a committee, see the [Chair Guide](../chair/getting-started) instead.

## Logging in

CHASE is a web app, so no installation is required, though a native desktop app is also available on some platforms. To get started:

1. Open the CHASE URL your conference organizers gave you (or the desktop app, if you were given an installer).
2. Log in through your conference's identity provider. Your conference administrators control who's allowed to log in. If you can't get in, contact them, not CHASE support.
3. You'll land on the **Launcher**. It shows one card per conference you belong to, with your role (for example **Delegation** with your country and committee), the dates, the location, the committees, and an **Open conference** button.

If you don't belong to any conference yet, the launcher says **No conferences yet**. Someone hasn't added you yet, so reach out to your conference's leadership or admins.

![The conference launcher, showing a conference card with role badge, committees and an "Open conference" button](shot:participant/launcher)

## Finding your committee

What happens when you click **Open conference** depends on your role:

- **Delegates** go straight to their own committee dashboard. If you haven't been assigned to a committee yet, you'll see a **Waiting for Assignment** screen until an admin seats you.
- **Non-state actors** and **spectators** first see a page with every committee in the conference, showing each one's name, abbreviation, active agenda item and current status (e.g. "Formal debate" or "Suspension"). Click a committee to open it.

Your role is set per conference by the conference's administrators:

| Role                      | What you'll see                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Delegate**              | Full participant view for the delegation you represent: speakers list, requests, voting, resolutions.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Spectator**             | Read-only view of the committee (speakers list, current status) without the ability to speak, vote, or submit papers.                                                                                                                    |
| **Non-State Actor (NSA)** | Represents an organization rather than a country. You can join speakers lists and send requests in any committee. You can't vote, write or sponsor papers, or propose amendments. Chairs check you in by scanning your printed QR badge. |

Even with a QR badge, NSAs log in like everyone else to use the speakers list and requests.

## Your committee dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

- **Identity card**: your country/organization's flag and name, so it's always clear who you're representing.
- **Committee status**: the current agenda item and committee status (e.g. "Formal debate", "Suspension"), with a countdown if the chair set a duration.
- **Majorities**: how many delegations are present, the votes needed for a simple and a two-thirds majority, and how many sponsors an amendment needs, updated live as attendance changes.
- **Speakers list** (delegates, NSAs and spectators): see [Speakers List & Speaking](./speakers-list).
- **Requests**: send requests to the chairs, if they've turned this on. See [Requests](./requests).
- **Resolutions**: a link into your committee's working papers and draft resolutions.
- **Whiteboard**: if the chair has shared something to the room's whiteboard, it appears here.

Everything on this dashboard updates live, so you don't need to refresh the page as debate progresses.

The bar at the bottom of the screen switches between **Committee** (this dashboard), **Resolutions** (the papers list) and **Currently Active**, the draft resolution being debated right now. **Currently Active** only appears while the chair has marked a draft resolution as active.

![A participant's committee dashboard showing the current status, majorities, speakers lists and requests](shot:participant/committee-dashboard)

## The menu and help

Tap your initials in the top right to open the menu. It shows your name, your role and the conference, and lets you:

- open **Statistics** (see [Your Statistics](./statistics))
- go back to the **Dashboard**, the launcher with all your conferences
- open **My Account**, your MUNify account page, in a new tab
- switch the theme and language
- **Sign out**

Delegates have no back arrow in the committee view, so use **Dashboard** in this menu to get back to the launcher.

![The menu open, showing the signed-in user's name, role, Statistics, Dashboard, "My Account", and "Sign out"](shot:participant/avatar-menu)

The **?** button next to your initials opens the manual page for the screen you're on.

## Staying connected

CHASE works offline-first. If your connection drops momentarily, a banner will let you know, and the app keeps showing the last known state rather than freezing or erroring. Reconnect and it catches back up automatically.
