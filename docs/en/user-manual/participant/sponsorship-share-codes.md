# Sponsorship & Share Codes

## Sponsoring a paper

Once a paper is **Submitted** (or still a Working Paper you've been invited into), you can back it as a sponsor. Open the paper and click **Sponsors** to see who's already sponsoring it and add your own delegation via **Sponsor this paper**. If you change your mind, remove your sponsorship from the same panel. A sponsor count is shown wherever the paper appears in your committee's list, so delegates can gauge support at a glance.

Sponsoring needs a seat in the committee, so non-state actors can't sponsor papers.

## Share codes

If you're the creator of a working paper, you can invite other delegates to help without needing the chair to add them manually, using **Share Codes** (available from the paper header while it's still a Working Paper):

- **Edit code**: lets whoever redeems it help write the paper alongside you.
- **Sponsor code**: lets whoever redeems it add their sponsorship to the paper.

Generate either from the **Share Codes** panel. Each appears as a short code you can send to the delegates you want to invite (e.g. via message or in person). A code keeps working for everyone you share it with until you delete it.

### Redeeming a share code

If someone gives you a share code, go to your committee's papers list and enter it in the **Enter share code** field, then click **Redeem**. Codes aren't case-sensitive.

Redeeming either code also adds your delegation as a sponsor. An **Edit code** additionally lets you write in the paper, and the paper then shows up under **My Papers**.
